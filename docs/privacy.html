Privacy Policy

Liquid Glass Keyboard · com.liquidglass.keyboard · last updated 28 October 2026

This keyboard collects nothing. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere — not even if it wanted to.

What the app can access

The app declares exactly one permission: VIBRATE, used for the haptic tap when you press a key. It does not request INTERNET, and there is no network code in the app.

What happens to what you type

  • Keystrokes go straight to the text field you are typing in, through Android's own input method interface. Nothing is written to storage, and nothing is logged.
  • The keyboard keeps no history, no dictionary of what you have typed, and no learned words. When the keyboard closes, nothing remains.
  • There is no clipboard access: the app never reads from or writes to your clipboard.
  • There is no predictive text, so text is never analysed to guess what you might type next.

What is stored on your device

Your preferences — theme, key height, key-click sound, haptics, and whether the background colour orbs drift — are stored in the app's own private settings on your device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Third parties

None. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics, no crash reporting, no attribution library, and no third-party code of any kind. The app is written entirely against the Android framework.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Open source

The complete source is public under the MIT licence at github.com/technicalnihilist-lgtm/liquid-glass-keyboard, so these claims can be checked rather than taken on trust. The build pipeline fails if the resulting APK ever requests internet access.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top will change with it, and the change will be visible in the repository's history.
