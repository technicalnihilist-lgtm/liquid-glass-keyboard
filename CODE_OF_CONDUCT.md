# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
condensed here so it is actually read.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and generous about people.
- Assume the other person is trying to solve the same problem you are.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change that does not fit the project's scope.
- Give and accept technical criticism without turning it into a judgement of the person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or comments, sexual attention or advances, deliberate
intimidation, publishing someone's private information without permission, and sustained
disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is representing
the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/technicalnihilist-lgtm/liquid-glass-keyboard/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message. Reports will be reviewed and answered. Maintainers may edit or delete
comments, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour they
judge harmful to the project.

The full Contributor Covenant 2.1 text — which this document follows, with the enforcement ladder
shortened — is available at the link above under CC BY 4.0.
